Joining

Use any Join button on this site to open our Discord invite. After joining, complete the onboarding and verification steps and read the rules.

The community has a Bangladeshi identity, but everyone is welcome. There is a Foreigner role you can pick in the self-role channel.

Some channels unlock after verification, by choosing roles, or by reaching a certain level. Check the self-role and requirements channels.

Roles & levels

Open the self-role channel and use the Take age role and Take gender role menus. Only your broad age group is assigned, so your exact age stays private.

Ping roles control which notifications you get (announcements, events, giveaways). Gaming roles show which games you play. Content roles unlock media-sharing channels and creator alerts.

Level 5 unlocks embed links and reactions in general chat. Level 10 lets you attach files and apply for staff. Level 15 adds external emoji and member-only activities. Levels 30 and 50 add event and VIP perks, and the top level unlocks the ultimate perks.

They are recognition roles. Ally goes to the monthly top three most active members, Crown to members who reach the highest levels, and Bhai Brother to consistently active members.

Regularly share your artwork or music in the art or music channels. These roles come with a showcase, a slowmode bypass and extra emoji access in those channels.

Boosting & perks

Boosters receive the VIP role with a booster-only channel, embed link and file permissions, booster-only giveaways, and external emoji and sticker access.

Server owners can get the Knight role, which includes networking with other owners and approved server promotion. Do not advertise without permission.

Help & safety

Open a ticket in the ticket channel or message a moderator. Please do not make false reports.

Reach Level 10, then check the requirements channel and apply in the apply-here channel.

Staff may take appropriate action, from a warning to a mute or ban, depending on the situation. See the Rules and Terms pages.
