SERVER GUIDELINES

KEEP IT
FRIENDLY.

A few simple rules keep NINETIES welcoming for everyone. Read them, follow them, and use common sense for everything else.

Violations may result in moderation action.

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness. No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.

Keep It Friendly

Avoid toxic behaviour, unnecessary drama, and starting arguments.

No Spam

No repeated messages, excessive mentions, or channel flooding.

Keep It Appropriate

No NSFW, offensive, or disturbing content.

No Advertising

Do not promote other servers, products, or social media without permission.

Listen to Staff

Follow moderator instructions and respect their decisions.

Have Fun

Enjoy the community and help make it welcoming for everyone.
