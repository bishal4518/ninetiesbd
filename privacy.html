What this website and our Discord community do, and do not, collect.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2026

1. Overview

NINETIES || 90's Bangladesh is a community run by volunteers. We try to collect as little information as possible. This policy covers our website and our Discord server.

2. This website

The website is a static site. It has no accounts, forms or comments, and we do not set our own cookies or run advertising trackers. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs under its own privacy policy.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads our typefaces, so your browser contacts Google's servers.
  • Discord: the Join buttons open Discord, and some pages may request our public server member count from Discord's invite API.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.

These services have their own privacy policies and we do not control them.

4. Discord server

Inside the server, Discord and the bots we use process data under Discord's Privacy Policy. Staff can see what you post in public channels, your username, your roles and your activity level. Bots may track levels, ranks and moderation history, and moderation actions may be logged to keep the community safe.

Roles such as age, gender, gaming and ping roles are chosen by you and are visible to other members. Please do not share passwords, personal addresses or other sensitive information in the server.

5. Age & minors

Members choose a broad age role (for example under 18 or 18+) so we can provide suitable content. We do not ask for your exact age or documents on this website. Minors should be careful about what they share and should never meet strangers from the internet alone.

6. Your choices

  • You can leave the server at any time.
  • You can change or remove your self-roles in the self-role channel.
  • You can ask staff to remove your own messages or roles by opening a ticket.
  • For data held by Discord, use Discord's own privacy tools.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open a ticket in our Discord server. See also our Terms of Service.
